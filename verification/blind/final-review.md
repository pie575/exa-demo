# Final blind visual review

Reviewed the four final A/B pairs after the native bottom controls and image
spacing corrections. The A/B identity key was not consulted.

| Pair | Which looks better? | Observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desktop homepage | Tie | Matching navigation, title, hero, examples, toolbar, and bottom control. |
| Mobile homepage | Tie | Matching wrapping, spacing, banner, controls, and lower-edge masking. |
| Desktop Search quickstart | Tie | Matching three-column layout, typography, code, controls, and lower edge. |
| Mobile Search quickstart | Tie | Matching content, wrapping, toolbar, bottom input, and masking. |

Neither version has an observable aesthetic advantage in these final images.
The separate pixel comparison confirms two pairs are bitmap-identical. The
other two differ by only eight and one pixels respectively at the recorded
per-channel threshold. These measurements describe the captured viewports.

Earlier reviewer observations and their corresponding screenshots are retained
in `review-round-2/`; those observations drove the final corrections.
